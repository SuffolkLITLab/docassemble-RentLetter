# Letter to a new landlord after foreclosure

Help a Massachusetts tenant ask the new owner where to pay rent after a completed foreclosure. The letter can also list needed repairs. The tenant prints, signs, and sends it. The interview does not pay rent or stop an eviction.

Entry point: `docassemble.RentLetter:data/questions/rent_letter.yml`.

This is an adaptation of MLRI's Conditions Rent Tender Letter (Form 26), guided by [MassLegalHelp's steps after foreclosure](https://www.masslegalhelp.org/housing-apartments-shelter/foreclosure/what-do-first). It asks for payment instructions; it does not tender payment or reproduce the original form verbatim.
